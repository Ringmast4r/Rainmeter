## Content

Version 1.1 adds the disc format names: CD-R, CD-RW, CD-ROM, DVD-R, DVD+R, DVD-RW, DVD+RW, DVD-R DL, DVD+R DL, DVD-RAM, DVD-ROM, BD-R, BD-RE, BD-R XL, BD-RE XL and BD-ROM. R means write once; RW/RE means erasable and rewritable; ROM means read-only. Plus and minus are different DVD recording standards, not different capacities. Hover each row for details.

DVD-RAM is rewritable: common 12 cm discs are 4.7 GB per side or 9.4 GB across two sides. BDXL write-once discs are 100 or 128 GB; BDXL rewritable discs are 100 GB.

CD 700 MB (80 min) and older 650 MB (74 min). DVD5 4.7 GB, DVD9 8.5 GB, DVD10 9.4 GB and DVD18 17.1 GB. DVD10/18 totals are across two sides.

Blu-ray single layer 25 GB and dual layer 50 GB; BDXL triple layer 100 GB and quad layer 128 GB (recordable BD-R). BDXL requires a compatible drive. Ultra HD pressed movie discs are a separate 50/66/100 GB reference.

Standard discs are 120 mm in diameter and 1.2 mm thick. Mini discs are 80 mm in diameter.
